Panels

Solar panel cleaning

Dust, salt, pollen, and grime can build up quickly. We clean panels gently so they look better and keep working efficiently.

  • Roof glass and skylight experience

Optional add-on

Hard water spot removal

Standard cleaning cannot always shift baked-on mineral deposits. This restoration service targets stubborn marks and clouding.

  • Assessed before work begins
  • Suitable for many mineral-marked glass surfaces
  • Quoted separately as an optional add-on

Optional add-on

Hydrophobic coating

A protective glass coating designed to repel water and help reduce grime build-up between scheduled cleans.

  • Helps water bead and run from glass
  • Makes ongoing maintenance easier
  • Available after suitable glass cleaning
