About us

Meet Vivan and Pablo.

Clarity Window Washing was founded by Vivan and Pablo, two driven operators who take pride in delivering reliable service and spotless results.

Vivan is a university student with a strong passion for business, customer service, and building something from the ground up. Pablo is a born entrepreneur, always looking for better ways to grow, improve, and deliver more value.

Together, we bring energy, care, and attention to detail to every job, helping homes and businesses look their best through crystal-clear windows.

Clarity Window Washing is fully insured and trusted by 500+ Auckland and Tauranga homeowners.
